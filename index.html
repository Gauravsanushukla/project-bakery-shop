15+ Years of Artisan Mastery

A Tradition of Slow Fermentation & Pure Passion

Founded by the Shukla family, our boutique bakery began with a simple stone oven and a commitment to European baking artistry. Every morning before 5:00 AM, our master bakers fold 27 layers of Normandy butter into laminated pastries and nurture 36-hour wild sourdough starters.

We strictly source unbleached organic flours, pure vanilla beans, and farm-fresh fruits. Zero artificial preservatives, zero shortcuts — only pure baked happiness.
