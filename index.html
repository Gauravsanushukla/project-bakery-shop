A Tradition of Flavor, Passion & Artistry

Founded by the Shukla family, our bakery began with a simple wooden oven and a passion for slow-fermented, stone-ground breads. Today, we continue to honor traditional European baking methods combined with modern culinary finesse.

We source only unbleached stone-ground flours, pure French butter, and locally sourced organic fruits to ensure every bite delivers an unforgettable experience.
